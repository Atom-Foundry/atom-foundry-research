# AI Knows Your Website

Recommendation Intelligence Research™ · Atom Foundry · July 2026

---

# Research Summary

How well do modern AI systems actually understand ecommerce websites?

This study investigates whether AI can correctly recognize, categorize and interpret an ecommerce business using only publicly available website information.

Rather than evaluating recommendations, the experiment focuses on an earlier stage of the AI decision-making process: understanding.

The findings demonstrate that AI forms semantic interpretations of websites before making recommendation decisions, making website understanding a fundamental prerequisite for AI-driven commerce.

---

# Research Question

Can AI accurately understand what an ecommerce business does without being explicitly told?

---

# Experimental Setup

- AI Model: GPT-4o
- Real ecommerce websites
- Public website content only
- Identical evaluation prompts
- Manual validation against the actual websites

Each website was evaluated using the same methodology to measure how accurately AI understood the business behind the domain.

---

# Key Findings

- AI can recognize many ecommerce websites without additional context.
- Website understanding extends beyond simple keyword matching into semantic interpretation.
- Recognition quality varies significantly depending on website clarity and structure.
- Missing, inconsistent or ambiguous information reduces AI's ability to correctly understand a business.
- Website understanding consistently precedes AI recommendation decisions.

---

# Why It Matters

AI cannot recommend a business it does not understand.

Understanding is the first stage of the AI Commerce Intelligence™ framework.

Before an AI system can evaluate trust, compare alternatives or generate recommendations, it must first determine what a business is, what it sells and who it serves.

This research demonstrates that website understanding forms the foundation of AI Readability™, AI Understanding™ and ultimately AI-powered recommendations.

AI recommendations begin long before a brand appears in an answer.

They begin with understanding.

Improving how AI interprets ecommerce websites may therefore have a greater long-term impact on recommendation performance than optimizing for recommendation prompts alone.
