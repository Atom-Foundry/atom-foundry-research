# Methodology

## Research Objective

Measure whether AI recommendation frequency is related to AI Commerce Score™ and store AI readiness.

## Dataset

* Category: Pets
* Model: GPT-4o-mini
* Shopping Intents: 20
* Runs Per Intent: 20
* Prompt Runs: 400
* Recommendations Captured: 4,000
* Distinct Brands Observed: 405

## Metrics

### Recommendation Share™

Percentage of all recommendations captured by a brand.

### Recommendation Frequency™

Percentage of prompt runs in which a brand appeared at least once.

### Recommendation Position™

Average position within the AI response when the brand appeared.

### AI Commerce Score™

Atom Foundry's proprietary measure of store AI readiness.

## Data Processing

Retailers and marketplaces including Chewy, Petco, PetSmart, Amazon, and Frisco were excluded from brand-level correlation analysis.

Brands were mapped to real stores where a verified match existed.

Brands without a verified store match were reported as off-index.

## Statistical Analysis

Pearson correlation was calculated between Recommendation Frequency™ and AI Commerce Score™ for all on-index brands.

Result:

r = -0.366

## Limitations

* Single model
* Single point in time
* Single category
* Recommendations vary across model runs
* Results do not imply causation

All findings are based exclusively on captured recommendation data.
