---

# Objective

Maintain a transparent public log of every meaningful change applied to Founder Lab and document its measured effects.

Unlike the research studies in this repository, this document is intentionally longitudinal and continuously updated.

---

# Research Question

How does Founder Lab evolve as changes are introduced one at a time?

---

# Experimental Design

Every meaningful intervention is recorded chronologically.

Each entry includes:

- the change performed
- the reason for the change
- validation
- follow-up measurements
- observed outcomes

Whenever possible, measurements are repeated to distinguish genuine changes from normal measurement variation.

---

# Dataset

Founder Lab Shopify store

Measurements include:

- AI Commerce Score
- Component scores
- AI bot instrumentation
- Technical observations
- Manual validation
- Infrastructure fixes

---

# Evaluation

Each log entry is independently verified before publication.

Infrastructure issues are documented alongside successful experiments to avoid survivorship bias.

---

# Metrics

Primary metrics include:

- AI Commerce Score
- Technical component
- AI bot tracking
- Instrumentation status
- Timeline progression

---

# Validation

Every published observation is supported by direct measurement, repeated scans, infrastructure validation or controlled testing.

---

# Limitations

This document is an operational research log rather than a finished experimental study.

Results should be interpreted together with the dedicated Founder Lab Day Zero baseline and subsequent Founder Lab studies.
