# Objective

Establish a reproducible baseline for Founder Lab before any optimization work begins.

Unlike observational studies, Founder Lab is fully controlled by Atom Foundry, allowing individual changes to be introduced and measured one at a time.

---

# Research Question

How stable is the AI Commerce Score when absolutely nothing changes on the website?

---

# Experimental Design

The identical Shopify store was scanned six times.

One scan was collected on July 12.

Five additional scans were collected within a 62-second window on July 17.

No website content, configuration, products, metadata or infrastructure changed between scans.

---

# Dataset

Store:

Founder Lab

Platform:

Shopify

Measurements:

- AI Commerce Score
- Component scores
- AI bot instrumentation
- Technical readiness

---

# Evaluation

Each scan recorded:

- Total AI Commerce Score
- Seven deterministic components
- Intent component
- Technical score
- AI bot activity

Component stability was compared across all six observations.

---

# Metrics

Primary metrics include:

- AI Commerce Score
- Component stability
- Intent variance
- AI bot visits
- Technical readiness

---

# Validation

All deterministic score components remained identical across every scan.

Only Intent varied, producing a total score range between 76 and 78.

The constant sum of every non-intent component demonstrates that observed variation originated exclusively from the model-evaluated Intent score rather than deterministic website measurements.

---

# Limitations

Founder Lab represents a single Shopify store and is intended as a controlled experimental platform rather than a representative ecommerce sample.

Bot-visit instrumentation is undergoing independent validation and should not yet be interpreted as complete evidence of crawler absence.
