# AI Commerce Vocabulary

Atom Foundry — Recommendation Intelligence Research (TM)
Published: 2026-09-18

A controlled vocabulary of 66 terms describing how AI systems discover, evaluate, trust, and recommend online stores and brands. Every term is drawn from Atom Foundry's public research program and is published as structured data (schema.org DefinedTermSet) on the live site.

Live version: https://atomfoundry.dev/vocabulary

## What this is

Atom Foundry runs public, repeatable experiments measuring how large language models and AI shopping agents evaluate and recommend brands, not just whether those brands are visible. Over the course of that research program a consistent set of terms emerged for describing the mechanisms involved, for example how confidently a model recommends a brand, how stable that recommendation is across repeated or reworded prompts, and what signals make a brand a credible candidate in the first place.

This vocabulary collects those terms in one place, each with a short working definition and a link to the full explanation on the Atom Foundry site.

## Files

- `ai-commerce-vocabulary.json` - machine-readable export, schema.org DefinedTermSet format (JSON-LD). Contains all 66 terms with name, definition, and canonical URL.
- `ai-commerce-vocabulary.csv` - the same 66 terms in a flat table (term, definition, url), for spreadsheet or non-JSON use.
- `CITATION.cff` - citation metadata.

## Scope and method

Terms are added to this vocabulary only after they are used consistently across Atom Foundry's published research and product pages. This is a working, descriptive vocabulary built from active research, not a formal ontology, and it will grow as new studies introduce new mechanisms worth naming.
