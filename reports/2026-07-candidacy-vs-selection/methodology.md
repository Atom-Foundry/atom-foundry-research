# Objective

Determine whether higher-quality ecommerce stores are more likely to be recommended by AI systems, or whether store quality primarily affects whether a store enters the recommendation candidate pool.

---

# Research Question

Does store quality influence recommendation selection, or only recommendation candidacy?

---

# Experimental Design

Unlike previous studies that analyzed only brands already recommended by AI, this study compares the entire scanned population.

Every store was assigned to one of two groups:

- Recommended at least once
- Never recommended

Average AI Commerce Scores and all score components were compared across both groups.

---

# Dataset

- AI Model: GPT-4o
- Stores scanned: 60,924
- Recommended: 599
- Never recommended: 60,325

---

# Evaluation

The analysis compared:

- Overall AI Commerce Score
- Seven score components
- Intent score
- Recommendation frequency
- Category robustness
- Brand ownership effects

---

# Metrics

Primary metrics included:

- AI Commerce Score
- Intent Score
- Component Differences
- Recommendation Frequency Correlation
- Explained Variance (R²)

---

# Validation

Additional robustness checks evaluated:

- Nine independent ecommerce niches
- Brand-owned vs independent stores
- Correlation with other score factors

---

# Limitations

The study measures observational relationships.

It cannot determine whether stronger intent causes recommendations or whether already-successful brands naturally develop clearer positioning.
