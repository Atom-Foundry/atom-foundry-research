# Objective

Measure how accurately modern AI systems recognize, understand and describe ecommerce websites when provided only with their domain.

---

# Research Question

Can AI accurately identify what a website sells, who it serves and how the business should be categorized?

---

# Experimental Design

AI systems were asked to analyze real ecommerce websites using only their publicly available domains.

No additional context, product descriptions or supporting information was provided.

The objective was to determine whether AI could correctly identify:

- Brand identity
- Business category
- Product offering
- Target audience
- Commercial intent

---

# Dataset

The experiment included real ecommerce websites across multiple industries.

Each website was evaluated using identical prompts and identical evaluation criteria.

---

# Evaluation

Responses were manually reviewed and compared against the actual websites.

Each response was evaluated for:

- Category accuracy
- Product accuracy
- Brand understanding
- Commercial understanding
- Overall interpretation

---

# Metrics

Primary metrics included:

- Website Recognition Rate
- Category Accuracy
- Product Accuracy
- Business Understanding
- Overall Interpretation Accuracy

---

# Limitations

Results represent AI behavior at the time of testing.

Future model updates may improve website understanding.
